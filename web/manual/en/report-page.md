Each smoke report has its own page, laid out like a product page. Click a report on the Smoke Reports page, a strain's page or a plant's **Smoke** step to open it.

![A smoke report's page: the photo, the strain and plant, the chips, the overall score, the facts, the buttons, and the Scores and Grow details cards](fig:smoke-report-top)

- {1} **Home grown** or **Dispensary**. With more than one photo, small thumbnails under the main photo switch between them.
- {2} **The strain**, its breeder and, for home grown, the plant and tent it came from. For bought flower, the dispensary shows instead. The breeder links to the strain's page when the strain is in your Strains, and the plant links to its page.
- {3} **Chips**: indica or sativa, photoperiod or autoflower, how it was smoked, and whether you'd grow or buy it again.
- {4} **The overall score** out of 10, with dots to show it at a glance.
- {5} **Facts**: THC, CBD, the date smoked and how long it cured. Bought flower also shows the price, amount and price per gram. Under them are the top effects and what it smells like.
- {6} **Edit** changes the report. **New report for** a plant starts another report for the same plant, for example later in the cure. **Delete** removes the report and its photos, after asking.
- {7} **Scores**: a bar for each score, with its weight.
- {8} **Grow details** for home grown flower: harvest date, tent, lights, container, medium, nutrients, germination to harvest, dry and cure time, and how it was smoked. For bought flower, this card is **Where it came from**: the dispensary, price, price per gram and batch.

## Further down

- **How it felt**: the effects and side effects.
- **Nose, flavor and buds**: smells, tastes and bud qualities.
- **Notes**.
- **This plant over time**, for home grown flower: a small chart of the plant's overall scores, one dot per report, so you can see how the cure changed it.
- **More reports**: your other reports for the same strain, from any plant or dispensary.
