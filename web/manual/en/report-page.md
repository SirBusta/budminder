Every smoke report features a clean, product-style profile highlighting tasting notes, sensory ratings and cultivation telemetry. Open a report from the Smoke Reports page, a cultivar profile or a plant's **Smoke** milestone node.

![A smoke report's page: the photo, the strain and plant, the chips, the overall score, the facts, the buttons, and the Scores and Grow details cards](fig:smoke-report-top)

- {1} **Source badge:** identifies whether flower was **Home grown** or purchased from a **Dispensary**. When multiple images are attached, a gallery strip appears below the main photo.
- {2} **Genetics & origin:** displays cultivar name, breeder and the originating plant and grow tent for home harvests. Dispensary reports display retail store origin. Breeder names link to your strain library, and plant tags navigate directly to that plant's grow log.
- {3} **Metadata tags:** indicate indica/sativa ratio, photoperiod type, consumption method and keeper verdict (grow or buy again).
- {4} **Overall score:** weighted score out of 10, accompanied by a visual rating bar.
- {5} **Spec sheet:** displays THC and CBD content, date evaluated and cure length. Dispensary reports display total price, package weight and calculated price per gram. Primary effects and aroma profiles appear below.
- {6} **Buttons at the top right:** **Edit** changes the report. **Export** saves it as an image or PDF (see [Exporting a report](#exporting-a-report) below). The **⋯** button opens a menu with **Delete**, which permanently removes the report and its photos after you confirm. For a report on one of your plants, the menu also has **New report for** *plant name*, which starts another report on the same plant to track how the scores change as the cure goes on.
- {7} **Category breakdown:** visual rating bars for bud quality, taste, smell and effects, along with their assigned weights.
- {8} **Cultivation context:** for home harvests, summarizes harvest date, tent zone, lighting, container, medium, nutrients, total cycle length (from germination, or from the cutting for a plant started from a clone), dry time, cure duration and consumption method. For dispensary purchases, this panel displays **Where it came from**, detailing store name, price, cost per gram and package batch code.

## Further down

- **Sensory and effect profiles**: the effects and side effects you selected.
- **Aroma, flavor and bud traits**: selected terpene descriptors, flavor notes and structural bud appeal.
- **Notes**: comprehensive subjective tasting notes, ash characteristics and cure observations.
- **This plant over time**: for home harvests, a chronological trend chart tracking how extended jar curing influenced overall scores.
- **More reports**: quick links to other smoke reports for this cultivar from across your garden or past dispensary purchases.

## Exporting a report

**Export** saves the report as a magazine-style page to share or print.

- **Style:** **Humidor** (dark brown and gold, with a framed photo) or **Botanical** (dark green, with the photo across the top). The previews show your report in each style.
- **Blend photo background into page:** appears only when the photo has a light background, such as a white backdrop. It turns that background cream, like a printed page.
- **File type:** **Image (PNG)** for sharing, or **PDF** for printing on US Letter paper. PDF pages keep a wide left margin for a three-hole punch.
- **Pages:** when the notes do not fit on one page, the report continues on a second page. For an image, choose **One tall image** or **Two images**.

Fields you hide in **Settings → Grow setup → Smoke reports** are left out of the export, as they are on the report page. For a report on one of your plants, Botanical also shows up to five of that plant's photos, each with its day and stage. The window remembers your last choices.
