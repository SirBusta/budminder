Every plant starts from a strain in your Strains. The strain brings its breeder, flowering time and the rest with it, and your seed count stays up to date. If the strain isn't in your Strains yet, add it there first. See [Adding a strain](/manual/adding-a-strain).

## Two ways to start

- On the **Plants** page, click **Add plant**.
- In the Seed Vault on the **Strains** page, click **Start a grow** next to a strain. The strain and a plant name are filled in, and **Decrease seed count by 1** is ticked.

## The Add Plant form

- **Plant Name**, for example *Blue Dream #2*.
- **Zone**: the tent the plant grows in. Choose **Add New Zone** to type a new one.
- **Strain**: start typing to search your strains.
- **Status**: the stage the plant is at now, for example Seedling for a plant you're adding a few weeks in.
- **Start Date**: the day it started. It's today unless you change it, and it can't be in the future.
- **Is Clone**: tick it for a clone, then pick its **Parent Plant** from the plants of the same strain.
- **Decrease seed count by 1** takes one seed out of the strain's Seed Vault count.

Click **Add Plant** and BudMinder opens the new plant's page.

## When the strain isn't there yet

Click **Strains** in the note at the top of the form. BudMinder keeps what you've already filled in. Once the strain is saved, click **Back to Add Plant** and you're returned to the form with the new strain chosen and everything else still there.

## Editing a plant

Click the pencil on the plant's page, or in its row on the Plants page. You can change its name, tent, strain, start date, description and whether it's a clone. After harvest, this is also where you enter the **Harvest Weight**, in grams. **Link sensors** connects sensors to this plant.

Click **Save Changes** when you're done.
