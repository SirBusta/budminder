There are two ways to add a strain: look it up on StrainCompass, or type it in yourself. Both are at the top right of the **Strains** page.

## Find on StrainCompass

1. Click **Find on StrainCompass** and type the strain's name, for example *Blue Dream*. Type at least 2 letters, then click **Search**.
2. Find the right one in the list. The breeder is shown under each name, which helps when several breeders sell a strain with the same name. Type more of the name to narrow a long list.
3. Click **Add**, then choose where it goes under **Add it to**:
   - **I have seeds** puts it in the Seed Vault. Fill in how many, the seed type, the date and where they're stored.
   - **I want seeds** puts it on your Wanted list.
   - **Just for reference** saves it under All strains only.
4. Click **Continue to review**.

The **Review Strain Info** page shows everything StrainCompass has for the strain: its breeder, parents, type, flowering time, THC and CBD, effects, flavors and terpenes. Change anything you like. Nothing is saved until you click **Save Strain**, and then the strain's page opens.

If CannaDB is turned on in **Settings → Integrations**, BudMinder also fills in anything StrainCompass didn't have from CannaDB. When CannaDB has a picture of the seed pack, the review page offers it: use it, upload your own, or choose **No image**.

> **Tip:** If you already have the strain, BudMinder says so before you add it again, with links to open it or add seeds to it. Saving a strain you already have from the same breeder updates it, and keeps your seed count and storage location.

## Enter manually

For a strain that isn't on StrainCompass, such as your own cross or a cut from a friend:

1. Click **Enter manually**.
2. Fill in the **Strain name** and **Breeder**. Start typing the breeder and BudMinder suggests names; pick one, or keep typing to add a new breeder.
3. Choose photoperiod or autoflower, and the flowering time in weeks if you know it, for example 8 to 9.
4. Choose where it goes under **Add it to**, then click **Add strain**.

The strain's page opens next. Click **Edit** there to fill in the rest.

## Editing a strain

Click **Edit** on a strain's page to change anything about it:

- its name, breeder, link and descriptions;
- photoperiod or autoflower, the indica/sativa ratio and the flowering time;
- THC and CBD ranges, effects, flavors, terpenes and what it may help with;
- heights and yields;
- its parents, picked from your strains or typed in;
- the seeds: count, type, date added, storage location and whether it's Wanted;
- the seed-pack picture.

Click **Save Changes** when you're done.
