The Strains page organizes every cultivar in your library, including seeds currently on hand, varieties on your Wanted list and cultivars saved strictly for reference. Click **Strains** in the top menu to open it.

![The Strains page: the add buttons, the three tabs, the filters, the summary and the strain cards](fig:strains-vault)

- {1} **Find on StrainCompass** searches public catalogs to import a strain with its lineage, flowering times and cannabinoid specs prefilled. See [Adding a strain](/manual/adding-a-strain).
- {2} **Enter manually** lets you add custom crosses, clone-only cuts or unlisted genetics by entering the details yourself.
- {3} **Tabs.** **Seed Vault** lists cultivars with active seed stock, **Wanted** tracks strains on your wishlist, and **All strains** shows your complete library, including depleted seed packs, clone-only cultivars and reference entries. Each tab displays an active strain count.
- {4} **Sort and layout.** Toggle between visual **Cards** and a tabular **List**, or sort by name, oldest seeds, seed count, pack price or flowering duration. The drop-down filters let you narrow the view by breeder, photoperiod type or seed variety.
- {5} **Summary**: displays your total seed count across all cultivars, the cost and value of the seeds you have left, and how many strains have seeds older than 2 years. See [Pack prices](#pack-prices).
- {6} **Seed count.** Click **−** when popping a seed and **+** when restocking. The card displays seed type and seed age, with the age turning orange after 2 years. Each pack with a price shows a green tag with its price and the date it was added. A freebie shows a blue tag.
- {7} **Start a grow** opens the Add Plant form pre-populated with this strain, with **Decrease seed count by 1** already ticked. **Restock** logs new seed arrivals. See [Seeds](#seeds) below.

Each card displays the strain's breeder, parental lineage, a short description, photoperiod type, flowering window, active plants in your tents, historical grow count and storage location. Click any strain name to open its profile.

## Seeds

Clicking **Restock** (or **Add seeds** for an empty entry) prompts for:

- **How many** seeds to add to the inventory;
- the **Seed type**: feminized, regular or clone;
- the **Added on** date. On a restock, the seed age keeps counting from the original date unless you change this date;
- the storage location under **Stored in**, for example *Jar 1 (fridge)*;
- optionally, the **Pack price**, and **Freebie** if you got the pack for free.

When you log the last seed from a pack, the strain automatically leaves the active Seed Vault while remaining accessible under **All strains**.

> **Tip:** Seeds older than 2 years are flagged on the Today dashboard as well, helping you plan runs around older stock before viability declines.

## Pack prices

Enter a **Pack price** when you add seeds, restock or edit a strain to keep track of what your seeds cost. Each restock adds a new pack with its own price and date. For a freebie, turn on **Freebie** and enter the usual price of the pack.

Once a pack has a price, the summary above the strains on the **Seed Vault** tab shows two totals:

- **Cost**: what you paid for the seeds you have left. Freebies are not included.
- **Value**: what the seeds you have left would cost to buy, including freebies.

Both totals go down as you use seeds. For example, if you paid $100 for a pack of 10 seeds and have 4 left, the cost shown is $40. When a strain runs out of seeds and you add new ones later, its earlier packs do not count toward the totals.

## The List layout

**List** displays your cultivars in a structured table: name, lineage, type, flowering duration, seed inventory, pack price, seed age and historical runs. Click any column heading to sort the table.
