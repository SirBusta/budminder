The Strains page holds every strain you've saved: the seeds you have on hand, the strains you want, and the ones you keep just for reference. Click **Strains** in the top menu to open it.

![The Strains page: the add buttons, the three tabs, the filters, the summary and the strain cards](fig:strains-vault)

- {1} **Find on StrainCompass** looks a strain up and adds it with its details filled in. See [Adding a strain](/manual/adding-a-strain).
- {2} **Enter manually** adds a strain by typing it in yourself.
- {3} **Tabs.** **Seed Vault** shows the strains you have seeds for, **Wanted** the strains you want to get, and **All strains** everything, including strains with no seeds left, grown from clones, or saved for reference. Each tab shows how many strains it has.
- {4} **Sort and layout.** Sort by name or with the oldest seeds first, and switch between **Cards** and **List**. The lists on the left filter by name, breeder, photoperiod or autoflower, and seed type, and offer more ways to sort, such as most seeds or shortest flowering.
- {5} **Summary**: how many seeds you have across how many strains, and how many batches are over 2 years old.
- {6} **Seed count.** Click **−** when you use a seed and **+** to add one back. Next to the count are the seed type and the age of the seeds; the age turns orange after 2 years.
- {7} **Start a grow** opens Add Plant with this strain, ready to use one seed. **Restock** adds a new pack. See [Seeds](#seeds) below.

Each card also shows the strain's breeder, parents, a short description, photoperiod or autoflower, its flowering time, how many of its plants are growing now, how many times you've grown it, and where the seeds are stored. Click a strain's name to open its page.

## Seeds

**Restock** (or **Add seeds**, for a strain with none) asks for:

- **How many** seeds to add to the count;
- the **Seed type**: feminized, regular or clone;
- the date the seeds were **Added on**, which sets their age. For a restock, the date stays the same unless you change it;
- where they're **Stored in**, for example *Jar 1 (fridge)*.

When you use the last seed, the strain moves out of the Seed Vault. It stays under **All strains**.

> **Tip:** Seed batches more than 2 years old are flagged on the Today page too, so you can plan to use them first.

## The List layout

**List** shows the same strains as a table: name, parents, type, flowering time, seeds, seed age and grows. Click a column heading to sort by it.
