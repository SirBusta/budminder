Photos are kept with the day they were taken, so they sit right next to that day's notebook entry.

## Adding photos

Upload photos from any of these:

- the camera button next to **Add activity** in a plant's notebook;
- **Add a photo** on a day that has no photos yet;
- **Add photos** on the Today page, or the camera button on a plant's card;
- the camera button in a plant's row on the Plants page.

Drag photos into the window, or click to choose them. You can add several at once. For each one, check the date and add a short description if you like, then upload.

BudMinder reads the date from the photo itself when the camera saved one. When it can't, it uses the day you had selected, or today, and outlines the date box so you can check it.

The newest photo becomes the picture at the top of the plant's page.

## Viewing photos

Click a photo to open it full screen.

![The photo viewer: the date, the arrows, Decorate and Delete photo, and the thumbnails](fig:photo-viewer)

- {1} **The date** the photo was taken, and which photo of that day you're looking at.
- {2} **The arrows** go to the previous and next photo. The arrow keys on your keyboard work too.
- {3} **Decorate** adds text and a logo to a copy of the photo. See below.
- {4} **Delete photo** removes the photo, after asking. This can't be undone.
- {5} **Thumbnails** of the day's photos. Click one to jump to it.

Close the viewer with the X at the top right, or press Esc.

## Decorate

Decorate puts text on a copy of the photo, ready to share. Your original photo isn't changed.

- **Top Text** and **Bottom Text**: pick the strain, the breeder, the plant's name, its day or week, or **Custom** to type your own.
- **Placement**: the corner for each text.
- **Select Font** and **Text Color**.
- **Select Logo**: adds a logo you've uploaded in **Settings → Appearance**.

Click **Decorate** to make the copy. It appears in the window. To keep it, right-click it and choose **Save image**, or press and hold it on a phone.
