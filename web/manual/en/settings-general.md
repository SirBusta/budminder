Click **Settings** in the top navigation menu to open system configuration. Settings are organized into dedicated sections in the left sidebar: General, Integrations, Grow setup, Photos, Appearance, Cameras, Backup & data and Logs. Use the top search bar to find any setting quickly by keyword. Every preference saves automatically in the background as you adjust it, so there is no need for a manual save button.

## Region & display

- **Timezone**: select your local timezone so timestamps and schedules align accurately regardless of where your server is hosted. Choose **Use system default** to match the host server's local time.
- **Measurement units**: toggle between **Imperial** (inches, feet, ounces) and **Metric** (centimeters, meters, grams). These units apply to cultivar height, yield tracking and sensor temperature readings. Existing values recorded in the alternate unit system convert automatically for display.
- **Language**: choose English, German, Spanish or French. This preference applies only to your active browser session, matching the globe dropdown in the top header.
- **Theme**: select **Auto**, **Light** or **Dark** for your active browser. Auto synchronizes the interface with your operating system's light or dark mode setting.

## Guest view

Enable **Enable Guest Access** to let visitors browse your plants, strain library, photo galleries and smoke reports without credentials. Guests have read-only access and cannot alter data or configuration. BudMinder requires a server restart for this toggle to take effect. See [Finding your way](/manual/finding-your-way#guest-view).

## Account

Click **Change username or password** to change what you sign in with. See [First login and your account](/manual/first-login#changing-your-username-or-password).

## Other sections

- **Integrations**: configure AC Infinity, Ecowitt, Home Assistant, StrainCompass, CannaDB and custom REST API keys. See [Integrations and API key](/manual/integrations).
- **Grow setup**: manage tent zones, logged activities, custom metrics, target VPD curves, breeders and smoke report fields. See [Grow setup](/manual/grow-setup).
- **Photos**: keep photos as uploaded or compress new plant photos, compare qualities and compress photos already in BudMinder. See [Photos](/manual/photos#photo-size-and-compression).
- **Appearance**: upload a brand watermark or logo used by the **Decorate** tool on photos. See [Photos](/manual/photos#decorate).
- **Cameras**: configure camera endpoints and stream grabs. See [Tent cameras](/manual/tent-cameras).
- **Backup & data**: manage database backups and retention policies. See [Backup and restore](/manual/backup-and-restore).
- **Logs**: view live application diagnostic events and access records. See [Troubleshooting](/manual/troubleshooting#the-logs).
