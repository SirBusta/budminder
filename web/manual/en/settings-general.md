Click **Settings** in the top menu to open it. Settings is grouped into sections on the left: General, Integrations, Grow setup, Appearance, Cameras, Backup & data and Logs. Type in the search box at the top to find a setting by name. Every change saves as you make it; there's no Save button.

## Region & display

- **Timezone**: your local timezone, so dates and times are right wherever the server runs. **Use system default** takes the server's own.
- **Measurement units**: **Imperial** (in, ft, oz) or **Metric** (cm, m, g). Used for strain heights and yields and for sensor temperatures. Values recorded in the other system are converted for display.
- **Language**: English, German, Spanish or French. This is for this browser only; it's the same as the globe menu at the top.
- **Theme**: **Auto**, **Light** or **Dark**, for this browser only. Auto follows your device's light or dark setting.

## Guest view

Turn on **Enable Guest Access** to let people browse your plants, strains, photos and smoke reports without logging in. They can only look; they can't change anything. BudMinder needs a restart for this to take effect. See [Finding your way](/manual/finding-your-way#guest-view).

## Password

Click **Change Password** to choose a new one. See [First login and password](/manual/first-login#changing-your-password).
