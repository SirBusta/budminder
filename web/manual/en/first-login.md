The first time you open BudMinder, it asks you to create your account: choose a **username** and a **password** (at least 8 characters), type the password twice, and click **Create my account**. You're signed in straight away.

There's no default password to look up: until the account exists, every page leads to this one. Set it up as soon as BudMinder is running, before anyone else on your network opens it.

> **Updating from before 1.5.6?** An install that still had the old first sign-in (admin / budminder) shows the same page once, so you can choose your own username and password. Your plants and everything else stay as they are.

## Staying logged in

Check **Remember Me** on the login screen to keep your session active on that browser for 14 days. If left unchecked, closing your browser will log you out automatically.

## Changing your username or password

Go to **Settings → General → Account** and click **Change username or password**. Change either one, or both, and enter your current password to save. A new password logs out all your other browsers and phones; changing only the username doesn't.

## Logging out

Click **Logout** at the right of the top menu.
