BudMinder is a grow journal that runs on your own computer or home server. It keeps your plants, their daily notes and photos, your strains and seeds, your sensor readings and your smoke reports in one place.

## What's in BudMinder

- **Today** shows what needs doing and how every tent is right now.
- **Plants** lists your plants. Each plant has its own page with its stages, photos and a notebook of everything you did.
- **Strains** holds every strain you grow or want to grow, with the seeds you have on hand in the Seed Vault.
- **Sensors** charts the readings from AC Infinity, Ecowitt, Home Assistant and your own sensors.
- **Smoke** is where you rate how each harvest, or each dispensary pickup, turned out.
- **Settings** is where you set BudMinder up: tents, units, sensors, cameras and backups.

## Using this manual

Pick a section on the left, or type in **Search the manual** to find a word anywhere in it. On a phone, choose a section from the list at the top.

Pictures show the part of BudMinder being explained. Numbers in green circles on a picture match the numbers in the text next to it. Click or tap a picture to see it full size.

> **Tip:** The **Help** button in the top menu opens the section for the page you're on. Click it on a plant's page and you land on **Plant page**.

## Where to start

New to BudMinder? Read [First login and password](/manual/first-login), then [Finding your way](/manual/finding-your-way). After that, a good order is:

1. Add your tents in Settings, under **Grow setup** (BudMinder calls them zones).
2. Add a strain on the **Strains** page.
3. Add a plant from that strain.
4. Connect your sensors: on the **Sensors** page, click **Manage sensors**.
