The Plants page lists every plant you've grown, with where each one is between planting and harvest. Click **Plants** in the top menu to open it.

![The Plants page: the tabs, the summary, the filters and one plant's row](fig:plants-list)

- {1} **Tabs.** **Growing** has your living plants, **Harvested** the ones drying, curing or finished, and **Dead** the ones that didn't make it. Each tab shows how many plants it has.
- {2} **Summary** of the Growing tab: how many plants are growing, how many are in flower, how many are due for harvest within 3 weeks, and how many haven't been watered in 3 days or more.
- {3} **Filter plants** finds plants by name, strain, breeder, tent or stage as you type. The lists next to it narrow the page to one tent, breeder or stage, and **Sort** orders it by name, newest or oldest, longest since watering, or soonest harvest. **Clear** removes the filters.
- {4} **List or Cards.** List shows a row for each plant; Cards shows photo tiles. Your choice and sort order are saved in your browser.
- {5} **Progress to harvest.** The bar runs from the start date to the harvest estimate, with the plant's stage and week, its day number and how many weeks are left. It's striped when there's no estimate yet. On the Harvested tab, the yield shows here instead.
- {6} **Days since watering and feeding.** These turn amber, then red, at the same points as the Today page.
- {7} **Log** opens the plant's page ready to log an activity. The camera button uploads photos, and the pencil edits the plant.

Plants with a smoke report show a small badge with the latest score. Click it to open the report.

Click a plant's name or photo to open its page. **Add plant** at the top right starts a new one. See [Adding a plant](/manual/adding-a-plant).
