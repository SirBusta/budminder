The Plants page displays your complete grow history, showing each plant's current stage from seedling to harvest. Click **Plants** in the top menu to open it.

![The Plants page: the tabs, the summary, the filters and one plant's row](fig:plants-list)

- {1} **Tabs.** **Growing** holds active plants, **Harvested** lists plants currently drying, curing or finished, and **Dead** archives plants that did not survive. Each tab displays an active plant count.
- {2} **Summary** of the Growing tab: how many plants are growing, how many are in flower, how many are due for harvest within 3 weeks, and how many haven't been watered in 3 days or more.
- {3} **Filter plants** finds plants by name, strain, breeder, tent or stage as you type. Dropdown menus beside it narrow the view to a specific tent, breeder or stage, while **Sort** orders the list by name, age (newest or oldest), longest since watering or soonest harvest. **Clear** resets all active filters.
- {4} **List or Cards.** List displays a detailed table row for each plant, while Cards displays visual photo tiles. Your view choice and sort order are saved in your browser.
- {5} **Progress to harvest.** The progress bar runs from the start date to the estimated harvest window, showing the plant's active stage, week, total day count and remaining weeks. A striped bar indicates that no estimate is set. On the Harvested tab, this area displays your final yield instead.
- {6} **Days since watering and feeding.** These indicators turn amber, then red, following the same time thresholds as the Today page.
- {7} **Log** opens the plant's page ready to log an activity. The camera button uploads photos, and the pencil icon edits plant details.

Plants with a smoke report show a badge with their latest score. Click it to open the report.

Click any plant's name or photo tile to open its page. **Add plant** in the top right starts a new plant profile. See [Adding a plant](/manual/adding-a-plant).
