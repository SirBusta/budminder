Today is the first page you see. It shows what needs doing, then every tent with its live readings, its camera and its plants. Click **Home** in the top menu to come back to it.

## Needs attention

![The top of the Today page: the Log activity and Add photos buttons, then the Needs attention list](fig:today-attention)

- {1} **Log activity** opens the log window for any plants: water, feed, train, a measurement or a note.
- {2} **Add photos** uploads photos to a plant.
- {3} **Each line is one thing to look at.** BudMinder lists:
  - plants not watered in 3 days or more (red after 6 days);
  - plants not fed in 10 days or more (red after 18 days);
  - plants without a new photo in 14 days;
  - sensors that stopped reporting, and AC Infinity, Ecowitt or Home Assistant when they stop answering or need you to sign in again;
  - seed batches in the Seed Vault that are more than 2 years old.
- {4} **The button on the right deals with it.** **Watered it** and **Fed it** open the log window with the plant and the activity already picked and today's date filled in. The other lines have a button or link for the next step, such as **Add photo**, **Check sensors**, **Sign in again** or **Open Seed Vault**.

When everything is fine, the list says **Nothing needs attention right now**.

## Tents

Each tent (zone) has its own panel with everything in it.

![One tent on the Today page: its readings, the camera picture and two plant cards](fig:today-tent)

- {1} **Readings** from the tent's sensors, such as temperature, humidity, VPD and CO₂, and whether the lights are on or off when an AC Infinity port controls a light.
- {2} **Charts** opens the Environment page for this tent.
- {3} **The camera**, if the tent has one: the latest snapshot and how old it is. Click **Live** to watch it.
- {4} **When it was last watered and fed.** These turn amber, then red, at the same points as the Needs attention list.
- {5} **The harvest estimate**, in weeks, for plants in Flower. It comes from the strain's flowering time. See [Plant page](/manual/plant-page#growth-stages).
- {6} **Water**, **Feed** and **Log** open the log window for this plant. The water amount you gave last time is shown, so repeating it is quick.

Click a plant's name to open its page, or the camera button on its photo to add photos. Plants that aren't in a tent are listed under **No tent**.

## Recent activity

At the bottom, the last few things logged for any plant. **Open notebook** shows every plant's notebook together.
