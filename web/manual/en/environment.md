The Environment page charts every sensor in a tent on one timeline. Click **Sensors** in the top menu to open it, or **Charts** on a tent on the Today page.

![The Environment page: the tent buttons, the readings, the time range, a marked moment and the first chart](fig:environment)

- {1} **Tents.** Click a tent (zone) to show its sensors.
- {2} **Readings** right now, one box per sensor. Click a box to hide or show its chart. When a sensor stops reporting, its box says when the last reading came in.
- {3} **Earlier and later.** The arrows step back and forward through time by the length of the range you're looking at.
- {4} **Range**: the last hour, 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days, or **Custom** for any dates and times you pick.
- {5} **The marked moment.** Click anywhere on a chart and a line marks that moment on every chart, with each sensor's reading at that time. Drag the line to move through the day. Click the × to clear it.
- {6} **Each chart** shows the sensor's reading now (or at the marked moment), and its lowest, highest and average over the range.
- {7} **Manage sensors** opens the page for adding and arranging sensors. See [Manage sensors](/manual/manage-sensors).

## Devices

When a tent has an AC Infinity controller, the bottom of the page shows its ports, such as fans, lights and humidifiers, with their power level over the same time range. **Off** means the port wasn't running.

## No sensors showing?

If a tent has no charts, its sensors either aren't in that tent yet or are set not to show on this page. Both are changed in [Manage sensors](/manual/manage-sensors).
