Below the stage track, every plant has a notebook: one entry per day, with what you did, your notes, your measurements and that day's photos.

![A plant's notebook: the toolbar, the entries grouped by week and the selected day's photos](fig:notebook)

- {1} **This plant / All plants.** Switch to **All plants** to see every plant's notebook together. See [All plants](#all-plants) below.
- {2} **Weeks / List.** Weeks groups the entries by stage and week. List shows them one after another, newest first.
- {3} **Filter** shows only days with the activities you pick, such as Water or Train, or only days with photos. Choose whether a day needs any or all of them.
- {4} **All photos** shows every photo of the plant, day by day. The book button on a day jumps to its notebook entry.
- {5} **Export** downloads the notebook as a spreadsheet (CSV) or an Excel file.
- {6} **Add activity** logs something new. The camera button next to it uploads photos.
- {7} **Each row is one day:** the date and day number, an icon for each activity, your note, and any measurements such as water amount or height. A camera with a number means that day has photos. **Photos only** means photos were added but nothing was logged.
- {8} **The day's photos.** Click a row to show its photos here. On a phone, clicking the camera opens them.

## Logging an activity

Click **Add activity**, or **Log** on the Today or Plants page.

1. Check the date. It's today unless you change it.
2. Tick every activity that applies, such as Water, Feed and Train.
3. Fill in the numbers some activities ask for, such as **Water amount** or **Height**. Boxes marked * are required.
4. Add a note for the day if you like, then click **Save**.

Each plant has one entry per day. If the day already has one, BudMinder says so and adds to it.

> **Tip:** The activities and measurements are yours to change. Add, rename or remove them in **Settings → Grow setup**.

## Changing or deleting a day

Click **Edit** on a row to change that day's activities, numbers or note. **Delete this day** removes the entry but keeps its photos.

## All plants

**All plants** shows every plant's notebook in one list. Choose which plants to include at the top: all growing plants, everything including finished plants, a whole tent, or single plants. Search your notes in the box that appears, for example *defoliated*.

Click **Add activity** here to log for several plants at once, for example watering a whole tent. It's saved on each plant, but shows as one entry. When you edit it, change it for all of them, or untick a plant to change it for the others only.

**Export** in this view downloads the notebooks of the plants you've chosen.
