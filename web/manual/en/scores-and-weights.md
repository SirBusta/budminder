Every smoke report has four scores from 1 to 10, and an overall score worked out from them.

![The Scores part of the smoke report form: four rows of 1 to 10, each score's weight and the overall score](fig:smoke-scores)

- {1} **Tap a number** to score it. The number you picked and the ones below it fill in, from light green to dark green. Tap it again to clear the score.
- {2} **The weight**: how much this score counts in the overall score.
- {3} **Overall**: updates as you score.

## The four scores

- **Bud quality**: how the buds look and feel. Uniformity, trim, color and cure.
- **Taste**.
- **Smell**.
- **Effects**: how strong they are and how long they last.

You don't have to fill in all four. A blank score is left out of the overall.

## How the overall score works

The overall score is the average of the scores you filled in, with each one counted by its weight, rounded to one decimal. With every weight at 1×, scores of 8, 9, 7 and 8 give an overall of 8.0.

The overall shows green from 8, amber from 6 and red below 6, on the Smoke Reports page and on each report.

## Score weights

Go to **Settings → Grow setup → Smoke reports** to set how much each score counts, from 0× to 5×. For example, set Effects to 3× if effects matter most to you. 0× leaves a score out of the overall completely.

Changing a weight updates the overall score of every report, old and new.

## Fields on the form

In the same Settings card, turn off the parts of the form you never fill in:

- **Grow info**: date harvested, grow space, lights, container size, medium, nutrients, germination to harvest, and dry and cure time;
- **Other**: how it was smoked, side effects, bud quality choices, grow or buy again, THC and CBD, and dispensary, price and batch.

A field that's off is hidden on the form and on reports. Anything you already entered in it is kept, and shows again if you turn the field back on.
