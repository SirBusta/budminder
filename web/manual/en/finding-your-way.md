Every page has the same menu at the top.

![The top menu: Home, Plants, Strains, Sensors, Smoke, Settings, Help and Logout, then the language and theme buttons](fig:top-menu)

- {1} **The pages.** Home is the Today page. Then Plants, Strains, Sensors, Smoke and Settings. The page you're on is green, with a bar under it.
- {2} **Help** opens this manual at the section for the page you're on.
- {3} **Language.** BudMinder speaks English, German, Spanish and French. The choice is saved in your browser, so each person can pick their own.
- {4} **Light or dark.** Switches between the light and dark themes. This is also saved in your browser. To follow your computer's setting instead, choose **Auto** in **Settings → General**.

## On a phone

BudMinder works on a phone the same way. The menu wraps onto two lines, and pages stack their parts one under another instead of side by side.

## Guest view

If guest view is turned on in **Settings → General**, people can browse your plants, strains, photos and smoke reports without logging in. They see the pages but can't change anything, and the menu shows **Login** instead of **Logout**. Turning guest view on or off takes effect after BudMinder restarts.
